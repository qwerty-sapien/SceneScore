## Implemented and tested

Ten separately runnable scene scripts use shared deterministic recipe/export code, stable object/sonic IDs, seed-controlled timing and palette, and matching hero contact/near-miss variants. Export includes saved `.blend`, evaluated world-space mesh measures/bounds/transforms, causal derivatives, all pair interactions, compact summaries, exact event/state queries, camera conventions, config/code hashes and bundle hashes. Static geometry features stay compact; full states stay local. Scripted geometry is visibly labelled synthetic; force/impulse and physical dynamics are not invented.

Contact logic distinguishes centre distance and signed primitive/AABB surface gaps. Contact onset/sustain/release is de-duplicated. Exact swept sphere tests catch tunnelling. Near misses require a local positive-gap minimum with approach/separation and an independent continuous-segment certificate; ambiguous sphere/box or box/box intervals suppress the near-miss claim. Other contacts remain substep-sampled, not continuous collision guarantees. `physical_impact=false` and `impulse_ns=null`; the tile cascade is stylized scripted sliding/drop choreography, not simulated toppling. The visual floor is excluded from interaction claims.

Read-only FastAPI router/health and `blender-summary-1` views are ready for integrator mounting under `/scene`. A generic service render capability remains unregistered pending shared cancellation/cache policy. Existing bundle reads work without Blender. Rendering stays an explicit serial trusted-script CLI with PID/PGID, timeouts, SIGTERM/keyboard cancellation and verified owned-group teardown.

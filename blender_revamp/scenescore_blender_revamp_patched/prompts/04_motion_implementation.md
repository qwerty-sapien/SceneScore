# 04: replace invalid choreography with credible causal motion

Depends on 01, 02 and 03. Own the agreed generation/dynamics scope. Use the chosen brief and acceptance ledger; choose the simplest adequate implementation from live repo evidence.

1. Implement a real physical scene: consistent metres/seconds/gravity, visible support surfaces with colliders, appropriate primitive/convex collision shapes, coherent mass/material response and applied or correctly handled transforms. Make the initial stack genuinely supported and stable. Use sensible collision margins, substeps, iterations, friction, restitution and sleep thresholds, verifying the resulting behavior rather than trusting settings.
2. Author the actor or mechanism's preparation, release and follow-through. Verify held-ball attachment and continuity across any parent/constraint/kinematic-to-dynamic handoff. Capture meaningful linear and, where relevant, angular release velocity. No hidden scale inheritance, zero-velocity reset or teleport at release. Discover a supported Blender implementation locally; do not invent rigid-body velocity properties.
3. Let the physical solver or independently validated mechanics determine unpowered flight, impact, collapse, secondary contacts and rest. No linearly keyed tower response, unexplained impulse, universal smoothstep or hand-inserted settling bounce. Tune a small, recorded set of initial conditions to obtain a readable result. Every energy input and later support has a cause.
4. Build hero/contact and near-miss from one scene and parameterized aim/launch setup. The tower's collapse must result from actual disturbance, never a variant flag scheduling a fall. Withhold launch in a causal control: the target remains stable. Resolve excessive spin, indefinite jitter, clipping and visible penetration before look development.
5. Warm up and evaluate simulations in a valid monotonic sequence, bake/cache appropriately, and reopen in a fresh process. Verify that export and render read the same finalized motion. Unbaked out-of-order frame queries can be misleading; demonstrate the actual evaluation behavior. Treat bake-to-keyframe output as derived simulation data, with provenance and sufficiently accurate interpolation, rather than newly authored physical truth.
6. Apply the shared approach to the ten-recipe inventory after the hero/control vertical slice works. Retain semantic IDs when appropriate. Give driven/orbital/choreographed scenes an understandable physical or intentional context; do not force every recipe into the same throw-and-tower plot. Keep legacy invalid examples segregated from accepted new assets.

Keep runtime and physics clocks coherent. Use the shortest motivated scene duration that preserves readable anticipation, action and aftermath; do not preserve 30 seconds merely to fit the old soundtrack. Fill time with motivated beats rather than slowing gravity. Any changed duration/timing must flow to exports and integration, and the finalized animation duration becomes authoritative for downstream music adaptation. Deliver low-resolution native-cadence motion previews and evaluated data. Stop at the physical gate when defects persist; do not hide them with materials, blur or camera cuts.

# 08: independently review the delivered artifacts

Depends on 07. Read-only production review. Use a separate context when supported and do not rely on the creator's self-rating.

Open the exact delivered hero, near-miss, relevant catalogue previews and their diagnostic passes. Inspect at native cadence and around every important release/contact/support transition. Recompute selected physical checks from finalized evaluated data and verify source/bake/render/export lineage. Test a clean reproduction of the hero/control workflow where practical.

Compare with the actual old-video failure clips and provided positive/negative examples. Require explicit answers:

1. Can the silent video communicate who or what initiates motion, what happens at contact or miss, and why the ending follows?
2. Are support, release, rigid shape, collisions, response and settling credible? Does a native-cadence view reveal failures hidden by the contact sheet?
3. Do the setting, camera, lighting, materials, silhouettes and timing clarify the action? Are there needless labels, dead time, tangencies, distracting props or unresolved visual artifacts?
4. Do the media, evaluated states, event schedule, browser assets and draft soundtrack refer to the same candidate? Are collision/Foley events synchronized and free of spurious near-miss impacts?
5. Are negative controls rejected, positive controls accepted, all claimed tests actually run, and remaining human/perceptual/hardware gates explicit?

Use a concise rubric with separate physical, causal, visual, temporal and integration judgments. A fatal physical or causal defect cannot be averaged away by good lighting. Each criticism uses `Observed -> Effect -> Correction`, with a frame/time and evidence path. Do not manufacture criticism or claim auditory evaluation without hearing the output.

For failed items, send minimal repair requests to the owning node and rerun affected gates within budget. Approval claims remain tied to the exact re-reviewed candidate. If bounded repair fails or perceptual tools are unavailable, deliver the best verified draft with a truthful failed/unverified status and a reproducible resume step.

Final handoff: changed source paths; before/after media; source `.blend` and bake location; reproduction commands; positive/negative-control counts; gate-by-gate results; tools/versions actually used; resource cleanup; unresolved risks. Do not end with only documentation, an attractive still or an untested claim that Blender now uses physics.

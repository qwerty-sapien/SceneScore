# Preregistered sampled media review

Reference: Shi et al., [Judging the Judges: A Systematic Study of Position Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge](https://aclanthology.org/2025.ijcnlp-long.18.pdf), Sections 2.1–2.2. This is an adaptation to SceneScore evidence, not a validated music-quality instrument or a replication of the paper's text benchmarks. Requested judge: **gpt-5.6-sol, xhigh**.

Freeze before judging: the 12 current complete music mixes in `artifacts/music/review-v2`, two current validated Blender contact/near-miss videos, two current arranger audition WAVs, and the completed Phase 3 draft AV preview. These are 17 distinct deliverable units. Stems, MIDI/JSON, stills, copied web assets and superseded development renders are supporting evidence, not extra independent works. The draft mix and its mux are one linked performance unit. The universe is the current review set, not every historical intermediate file.

Draw 4/17 = **23.53%**, with two music units, one scene and one preview, uniformly without replacement within each stratum. Generate one random seed, record it and the sorted universe plus SHA-256 hashes in `sample.json`; never redraw after seeing results. Allocation guarantees coverage but is not a self-weighting estimate: per-stratum inclusion probabilities differ. No whole-catalog success rate will be inferred from four works. Supplemental comparators must be exact duplicates of selected units or other selected units; do not quietly expand the review sample.

Evaluate each sampled unit with its actual media and supporting symbolic/geometry data. Criteria: original editable motifs and coherent form/harmony; predominant blues/ragtime swing with restrained bossa and selective variation; persistent object voice/register; signed approach/separation/near-miss response; exact contact Foley and absent near-miss contact; temporal/duration/peak/decoding correctness; honest provenance. The explicit creative traits in VISION govern, not artist-name similarity or copied tunes. Human auditory quality, aesthetic preference, clicks/balance and expressive feel remain unscored unless the judge actually has a usable audio perception tool. Statistics or reading note names are not hearing. Still-frame inspection does not establish full-motion smoothness or physical AV synchronization.

Per-criterion outcomes: evidence-supported pass, evidenced defect, not applicable, or not assessable. Record exact file hashes, commands and modalities actually examined. Do not approve a human plan or clear AUDITION_PENDING. Preserve negative results and blockers.

Position-bias procedure: pair the two sampled music units on their shared technical/symbolic criteria. For the two remaining units compare only genuinely common visual or technical criteria; if incompatible, use blinded exact-duplicate control pairs and report their limited purpose. Candidate names, variant labels, prior quality reports and implementation conclusions must be hidden from trial judges. A coordinator can create neutral packets from objectively extracted evidence; archive extraction code and note that metadata may still reveal identity.

Run each original and swapped query **three times in fresh judge contexts**, with identical rubric and A/B/C (tie) choices. Use `NOT_ASSESSABLE` outside that choice set when required modalities are absent; exclude those from preference metrics but report counts. Independent fresh contexts are essential: repeating in one conversation is not valid repetition-stability evidence. If unavailable, mark metrics NOT_RUN rather than inventing them. Small blinded duplicate controls test judge consistency, not artistic merit.

Metrics: RS is mean modal-choice fraction across repetitions of each identical query. PC is the proportion of valid original/swapped pairs that identify the same underlying winner (or tie in both). Count primacy pairs AA/AC/CA and recency BB/BC/CB separately. PF_raw = rcn×irr − pcn×ipr, with rates among inconsistent pairs; define zero when there are no inconsistent pairs. For N valid pairs, use attainable bounds −N/+N in the paper's min–max expression, giving PF = PF_raw/N. Report raw counts, valid denominators, ties and missing judgments; tiny-sample descriptive metrics do not certify unbiasedness or music quality. No listwise experiment is claimed.

Evaluation owns `reports/media-evaluation/` and ignored `artifacts/media-evaluation/` only. Application/media source bytes are frozen during review. No training, media generation, provider calls, human approval or Phase 4 work. Frame extraction and objective decoding of existing media are permitted. Stop all task-owned analysis jobs before handoff.

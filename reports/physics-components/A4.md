# A4 — Event-plan and feasibility validator

PASS within the authoring scope. JSON/YAML input produces the required paired
artifacts, with explicit reasons for rejection and builder-consumable constraints.
The existing typed A3 intent is embedded in a separate versioned wrapper; canonical
contracts and the original schema remain unchanged.

Implementation adds `modules/blender/planning/{contracts,validator,io,__main__}.py`
and its public exports, a complete JSON fixture/structural schema, focused tests
and `docs/PLAN_VALIDATION.md`. Constraints bind to the complete original input and
are recomputed before use. The builder receives actor identities, numerical event
predicates, route states, materials, camera/ending intent, reference lessons,
continuous support and proposed music hints.

The gate rejects contradictory dependencies/windows, missing participants or
supports, absent/invalid success criteria, missing physical near-miss ranges,
clearance refinements excluding the declared target, unsupported mechanics,
manual post-release motion, teleportation and undefined terminal states. Contact
success requires a new onset in the event window. A collision whose entire window
is already continuous support is rejected; support samples stay out of the sparse
music stream. A3 unary settle resolves to its terminal catch support without
changing A3's participant contract. Conservative energy and deadline checks are
necessary bounds, not trajectory solutions.

Warnings expose nearly planar or spatially clustered routes, uniform spacing,
weak endings and incomplete camera exposure. The threshold policy and all six
planning answers appear in the report. Unknown camera references fail; missing
coverage/focus produces warnings without mutating the input.
